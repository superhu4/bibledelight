([source](https://redeemingproductivity.com/macarthur-bible-reading-plan/))

## Feature

- Read a short New Testament book (or section of a longer book) each day for 30 days.
- (See Reagan Rose's post and worksheet [here](https://redeemingproductivity.com/macarthur-bible-reading-plan/).)


## My Thoughts
- Like the [5x5x5 New Testament Plan](/plans/5x5x5), this also a very accessible and manageable way to read through the New Testament.
- However, this goes through at a much deeper level.
- Obviously, this lacks any Old Testament exposure and doesn't align with the church calendar — unless you carefully arrange it (e.g., read a Gospel towards the end of Advent and Acts after Easter).
